🧠 About IESTM

Learn more about IESTM and its development path

IESTM Company is an independent software brand founded by Radmehr Asghari. This organization aims to develop practical software, digital games, web‑based tools, and modern interactive experiences.

IESTM's main focus is on web development (HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Three.js), desktop programming (Python, PyQt, Tkinter), and user experience design. All projects are built with a creative approach, high performance, and elegant design.

IESTM is not just a company; it is a personal journey to learn, build, and deliver digital products that can make an impact in daily life, education, entertainment, and productivity.
